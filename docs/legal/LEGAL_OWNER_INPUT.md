- Governing law and dispute-resolution terms.
- Public legal/contact URL. (support@vibe-groups.com exists; entity details, address, governing law, and minimum age do not.)

- Countries and availability.
- Moderation owner, escalation path, and response targets.
- App Store seller name and copyright holder.
- Export-compliance determination (see docs/app-store/EXPORT_COMPLIANCE.md).

- Demo/review account decision for App Review.
